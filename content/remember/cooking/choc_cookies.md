# Ingredients

- 250g butter
- 125g sugar
- 125g molasses or similar
- 2 eggs
- 350g flour
- 1 coffee spoon baking powder
- 1 coffee spoon baking soda
- chocolate of choice, amount of choice and whatever you want

# Preparing the dough

1. Melt the butter and add the sugar, mix until smooth
2. Add the eggs, mix until smooth
3. Add the flour and baking soda and powder, mix until smooth

# Baking

Bake at 185C for 10 to 15 minutes depending on how soft you like your cookies.
